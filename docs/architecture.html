Dollar Hunter

Architecture and logic, end to end. A grocery-savings assistant that works entirely by text message.

1. The system at a glance

A customer texts a phone number. Everything else happens on one small server: the message is stored, an AI agent works out what they mean, code looks up this week's flyer prices near them, and a quote goes back by SMS.

3. Where the data comes from

Prices are gathered ahead of time, so answering is a database lookup, not a slow web search.

flowchart TB PC["Postal code from the customer
(K2C 0C1, or just K2C)"] --> GEO["GeoNames postal areas
bundled with the app"] GEO --> AREA["Area: Ottawa, Carlington
lat / lon"] AREA --> NEW{"Catalog for this area
fresh (under 12 h)?"} NEW -- "no" --> BG["Background download
(never blocks a reply)"] BG --> LIST["Flipp: list every grocery
and pharmacy flyer for the area"] LIST --> ALL["Download each flyer in parallel
about 9,000 items, 3-5 s"] ALL --> CAT[("flyer_items table
store, product, price,
regular price, valid dates")] NEW -- "yes" --> CAT CAT --> SEARCH["Item search: whole-word match on the
agent's keywords, English + French"] SEARCH --> HIT{"Found in a flyer?"} HIT -- "yes" --> CAND["Candidates per store"] HIT -- "no" --> ONLINE["Live search: regular online prices
(mostly walmart.ca)"] ONLINE --> HIT2{"Found?"} HIT2 -- "yes" --> CAND HIT2 -- "no" --> WEBP["Firecrawl reads store websites
(Metro, No Frills), capped per day"] WEBP --> CAND AREA -.-> OSM["OpenStreetMap: store names and
addresses, background only"] CRON["Worker, hourly"] -.-> NEW
  • Regular price comes from the flyer's own discount (a 36% off item gives its regular price), so savings are checkable. Items without it carry no saving and no fee.
  • Caching: flyer catalog 12 h per area, live searches 6 h, website prices 3 days, store lists 7 days, Jev decisions 6 h (the same basket always gets the same picks).
  • Honest limits: Flipp has no official API; this uses the endpoints its own app uses. Store websites show their default branch.

5. From basket to quote

flowchart TB B["Basket lines
name, size, brand,
keywords, details"] --> S["Search local catalog
per line"] S --> J["Jev: for every line and store,
which product is it? (one request)"] J --> M["Best match per
item per store"] M --> P["Trip planner: every 1-3 store
combination; each item at the
cheapest store in the trip"] P --> SC["Score = total + $3 per extra stop
(or cheapest / fewest / one store)"] SC --> Q["Quote: lines per store, flyer link per
store, savings vs regular, alternatives"] Q --> SMS([SMS])
  • Savings = regular price minus flyer price, only where the flyer states the regular price. Fee = 25% of verified savings; none without them.
  • Alternatives shown with every quote: buying everything at one store, the absolute cheapest spread, or the fewest stops, so the customer sees the trade-off.
  • Texts are kept in the GSM-7 alphabet (symbols such as ® removed, bilingual names reduced to English), which keeps the number of billed SMS segments down.

"Clear my list" empties only the basket. The old list stays as previous list, so the agent can say what was on it and add items back on request; preferences, location and history stay.

  • No ports published: traffic arrives only through the Dollar Hunter Cloudflare tunnel; nothing else on the host is touched.
  • Self-cleaning: logs capped at 10 MB per container, receipts deleted after 7 days, finished jobs after 14 days, messages after 90 days, previous and current image kept for rollback.
  • Removal: one script deletes its containers, volumes, images, build cache, timer and folder.
  • Costs per message: chat model about $0.002, Jev about $0.0004, a receipt under $0.001, a quote 3 to 5 SMS segments.
